The `http_client` and `prometheus_scrape` sources now support following HTTP redirects through a new `redirects` option. The `prometheus_scrape` source follows redirects by default, which matches the behavior of Prometheus, while the `http_client` source does not, which preserves the behavior of existing configurations. The scrape timeout applies to the entire redirect chain, 301, 302, and 303 responses switch requests other than `GET` and `HEAD` to `GET` while preserving the body of existing `GET` requests, and 307 and 308 responses preserve the method and the body. For cross-origin redirects, credential headers are not forwarded by default unless the `redirects.cross_origin.not_forward_headers` option is configured otherwise. The `prometheus_scrape` source is an exception: it forwards `Authorization` across origins to match Prometheus behavior, so credentials may be sent to the redirect destination.
